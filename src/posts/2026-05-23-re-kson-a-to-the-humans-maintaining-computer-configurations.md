KSON: A 💌 to the humans maintaining computer configurations https://kson.org/  <div class="markdown"><blockquote>
<p>Kson is a superset of JSON</p>
<p>KSON combines the strengths of YAML and JSON</p>
<p>With KSON you can write files that are easy to read, edit, and debug—without worrying about invisible whitespace errors or missing commas.</p>
<p><strong>YAML promises human-friendly config</strong>, but in practice <a href="https://noyaml.com/">we struggle</a> with whitespace sensitivity, poor error feedback, and opaque advanced features.<br />
<strong>JSON solves some of these problems</strong> but introduces its own: excessive quotes, no comments, strict comma rules, and nested structures that complicate diffs.</p>
<p><strong>KSON offers the best of both worlds:</strong> the readability of YAML with the robustness of JSON—and more.</p>
